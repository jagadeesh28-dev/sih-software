# Slide 2: The Egreen Quanta Solution

## Core Engineering Concept
A **Controlled Maritime Decision-Support Platform** combining real-world sensor telemetry, physics-informed safety boundaries, classical quantum-inspired search mechanisms, split conformal uncertainty quantification, and fleet-level multi-objective optimization.

---

## Key Pillars of the Platform

1. **Grounded Empirical Telemetry**:
   - Built and validated on $173,974$ real operational records across 3 distinct commercial vessels.
   - Zero synthetic interpolation in baseline models.

2. **Defense-in-Depth Safety Architecture**:
   - Multi-stage input validation, physical plausibility checks, and Out-of-Distribution (OOD) envelope guards.
   - Fault-tolerant fallback routing to reference models and emergency physics.

3. **Quantum-Inspired Feature & Hyperparameter Search**:
   - Classical QIEA ($Q$-bit probability vectors and rotation gates) for feature selection.
   - Quantum-Behaved PSO (QPSO) for hyperparameter tuning.
   - **44.7% higher population exploration diversity** with statistical parity to classical controls ($p = 0.684$).

4. **Split Conformal Uncertainty Bounds**:
   - Rigorous distribution-free, finite-sample $90\%$ prediction intervals.
   - **31.17% sharper intervals** while preserving $93.56\%$ empirical coverage.

5. **Fleet-Level Multi-Objective Optimization**:
   - Multi-vessel, multi-leg voyage scheduling balancing fuel consumption, voyage costs, port arrival deadlines, and CII ratings.
   - Distinct separation of penalized objective ($873.23\text{ t}$) from pure physical minimum ($3.24\text{ t}$).

6. **Alternative Fuel Scenarios**:
   - Transparent thermodynamic scenario modeling (invariant shaft work energy basis) for Bio-Methanol, Green Ammonia, and Liquid Hydrogen.
