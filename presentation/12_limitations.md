# Slide 12: Remaining Limitations & Scientific Disclosures

## Transparent Research Limitations (Non-Negotiable)

1. **Three Real Vessels**: Empirical validation is based on three specific ship hulls (two passenger cruise, one offshore supply vessel).
2. **Limited Cross-Vessel Generalization**: Direct zero-shot transfer across completely unmodeled hull forms (e.g., container ships or bulk carriers) requires vessel-specific recalibration.
3. **Alternative Fuels Are Scenario-Based**: Bio-Methanol, Ammonia, and Hydrogen rates are thermodynamic scenario simulations ($E = P_B \cdot t$), not measured telemetry.
4. **Synthetic OOD Scenarios**: While in-domain data is 100% real, extreme hurricane states ($H_s > 10\text{ m}$) are synthetic boundary simulations.
5. **Substantial Prediction Uncertainty**: During high-acceleration or severe maneuvering, conformal interval widths can exceed $1,500\text{ kg/h}$.
6. **Competitiveness, Not Superiority**: QI-C1 is statistically competitive with classical GA ($p = 0.684$); it is not proven universally superior.
7. **Negative Finding (MPS Divergence)**: Direct Matrix Product State (MPS) tensor network prediction was unviable under the tested configuration and was excluded from serving.
8. **Optimization Depends on Assumptions**: Optimal voyage speeds depend heavily on fuel prices, charter rates, and laytime contract penalties.
9. **Decision Support Prototype**: The platform provides advisory recommendations for human masters and onshore fleet managers.
10. **Not Certified for Autonomous Operations**: Lacks DNV / IMO Type Approval for autonomous vessel steering or throttle control.
11. **Further Real-World Validation Required**: Requires multi-season operational trials across diverse global shipping lanes.
