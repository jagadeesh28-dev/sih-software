## Thermodynamic Conversion Basis
To deliver identical brake power $P_B$ to the propeller shaft over voyage duration $t$:
$$E_{\text{shaft}} = P_B \cdot t = m_{\text{fuel}} \cdot \text{LHV}_f \cdot \eta_f$$
$$\dot{m}_f = \frac{P_B}{\text{LHV}_f \cdot \eta_f}$$

## Emissions Accounting Integrity
- **Tank-to-Wake (TtW)**: Direct stack emissions from vessel internal combustion.
- **Well-to-Tank (WtT)**: Upstream feedstock production, synthesis, and bunkering energy.
- **Well-to-Wake (WtW)**: Complete lifecycle carbon footprint per IMO LCA / FuelEU Maritime standards.
- Prevents greenwashing by capturing upstream electrolysis and transport penalties.
